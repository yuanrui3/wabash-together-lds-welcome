Why share a ride?

An empty seat on the way to work can help a neighbor get to a shift. Sharing a car can help a family put off buying a second one. When two commuters make one trip together, they use one parking space at their destination.

Paul asks us to “bear ye one another’s burdens” (Galatians 6:2). King Benjamin asks us to “impart of your substance to the poor,” each according to what we have (Mosiah 4:26). Sometimes what we have to offer is the ride we are already taking.
